# Cut shorts from a long video

## Use this when

The channel has a long video of its own and wants short videos from it. Only for the channel's own videos, or ones it has clear permission to use.

## Steps

1. **Get the words with times.** Use the script if you have it, or make a transcript from the sound.
2. **Mark every moment that could stand alone:** a question raised, a turn, a surprise, a strong line, a result.
3. **Rate each moment** from 0 to 2 on:
   - **opening:** does it start on something that makes a stranger stay?
   - **stands alone:** does it make sense with nothing before it?
   - **worth it:** does the viewer get something by the end?
   - **energy:** does something happen?
   - **fit:** does it suit the platform's length and shape?
4. **Drop anything a new viewer would not understand,** however good it is in the full video.
5. **Spread your picks** across the video. Three clips from the same two minutes are one clip.
6. **Give each clip its own opening** with the `hook` skill. The first second of a short is rarely the first second of the scene.
7. **Re-cut for the tall shape.** Check every shot after cropping (`make-narrated-video`, the step on tall versions).
8. **Point back to the full video** the way the platform allows, by its plays.
9. **Each short is its own experiment.** Bet, ship, measure. Note in the experiment which long video it came from.

## Checks before you call it done

- Each clip was shown to fresh eyes with no knowledge of the long video, and they understood it.
- No two clips come from the same moment.
- Each clip is different enough from the others that it is not the same post again.
- You own the source or have permission.

## Good and bad

- Good: a 25-second clip that opens on "So why did it fail?", shows the one reason, and needs no earlier scene.
- Bad: the first 60 seconds of the long video, uploaded tall.
