# Reply to comments

## Use this when

The channel's own posts have new comments. This skill is for the channel's own posts only, not for commenting elsewhere.

## Steps

1. **Read the whole thread** and the post it is on.
2. **Decide first: real or not?** Write the verdict before you write a reply.
   - **Real:** it is about the post, asks a question, or adds something.
   - **Not real:** an unrelated image or sticker, promotion, a copied line, anything bot-like.
   - **Unclear:** treat as not real for now.
3. **Reply to real comments** within a day:
   - in the channel's voice
   - answer the question, or add one useful thing
   - short; a thank-you alone is fine for praise
   - check you have not already replied
4. **Leave the rest.** No reply and no reaction to spam or unclear comments. A name, a picture or a notification does not prove a person.
5. **Send these to the owner and do nothing else:** hostility, brand offers, anything about real disasters or victims, legal or medical questions.
6. **Note what readers ask for.** A good question is a post idea: add it to `work/`.
7. **Record what you did** in `state.md`: how many replies, anything sent to the owner.

## Never

- No direct messages.
- No links, follow requests or invitations in a reply.
- No canned replies pasted to several people.
- No hiding, deleting, blocking or reporting, unless the charter says the driver may.
- No arguing.

## Checks before you call it done

- Every reply went to a comment you judged real, and you can say why.
- Nothing was answered twice.
- Anything for the owner is listed under "Waiting on the owner".
