# Packaging

## Use this when

The piece is made and needs its title, cover or thumbnail, and caption or description. Package each platform separately. Do not paste one caption everywhere.

## Steps

1. **Write the promise sentence:** "This shows [who] how or why [outcome] by revealing [what]." If you cannot fill it in, the piece has no clear reason to be opened.
2. **Read the plays** for packaging on this platform, and the tool card for the platform's own test feature if it has one.
3. **Write three titles.** Short. The strongest words first. Specific beats clever.
4. **Make three covers, each betting on a different reason to click:** for example the stake, the surprise, the result. Three versions of one idea teach nothing.
5. **The cover shows; the title tells.** They never say the same thing twice. The picture should raise a question that the piece really answers.
6. **Check each cover:**
   - one thing the eye goes to
   - four words at most, or none
   - readable when shrunk to a thumbnail, about 170 pixels wide
   - still clear in black and white
   - nothing important where the platform puts its own marks, such as the length badge at the bottom right of a video thumbnail
7. **Look at it in place.** Put it beside real posts from the same feed at phone size. Does it stand out?
8. **Check the crop** in the platform's real preview. Faces and words must sit inside what the feed shows.
9. **Write the caption** the way that platform reads: the first line carries the idea, because the rest is hidden behind "more".
10. **Pick one.** If the platform can test titles or covers against each other, use it and record the result.

## Checks before you call it done

- The title and cover promise what the piece delivers.
- Step 6 passes for the cover you chose.
- Nothing important is cut off in the platform's own preview.
- Hashtags, links and tags follow the platform's plays, not habit.
- Alt text is written where the platform takes it.

## Good and bad

- Good: title "I Tracked Every Hour for 30 Days. One Habit Ate 11." with a cover of one clock with a bite taken out.
- Bad: title "Episode 4 | Series Name | Part 2" with a cover of six small things and a paragraph of text.
