# Model a proven post

## Use this when

You are about to make a piece. A post that already worked for an account like yours is better evidence than your own guess. Start from one, make your own version, and let the owner see the two next to each other.

The 1-in-5 untested slot is the exception. There, write `none: <what you are testing>` as the reference.

## Steps

1. **Pick the reference** from `work/outliers.md`: a row at 2× its account's usual or more, on your platform, in a format you can make, from the last year. Prefer a pattern that shows in three rows or more. No row fits: run `find-what-works` first. Do not pick from memory.
2. **Take it apart on six lines.** Write what you see, not what you think of it:

   | Line | Write down |
   |---|---|
   | Opening | The first line, or the first second, word for word |
   | Shape | The order of its parts |
   | Size | Words or seconds, and how many pictures |
   | Look | The kind of picture, how much text sits on it, a face or none |
   | Feeling | What the reader feels, and at which point |
   | Passing it on | Whom a reader would send it to, and what sending it says about them |

3. **Mark each line keep or change.** Keep the driver (the one thing that made it work, from `find-what-works`), the shape and the size. Change the subject matter, the words, the story and the picture. **Never take their look.** The art style, characters, colours, type and layout always come from your own `look.md`. A follower must know the piece is ours.
4. **Add what only this channel has.** Its point of view, from the charter. Its own material: something that happened, a reader's question, its own numbers. If the reference account could post your piece unchanged, it is not yours yet. If you have no real material for this one, say so and pick another reference. Do not invent a scene and present it as real.
5. **Make it** with the skills for the format (`hook`, `write-post`, `make-image`, `script-short-video`).
6. **Copy test.** Put the two next to each other. The mechanism is the same; everything else is different. Take out any sentence, title, picture or story that is theirs. Someone who knows the reference should think "the same kind of post", never "that is theirs".
7. **Write the pair on the review sheet,** `work/review/YYYY-MM-DD.md`. One block per piece, stacked so it reads on a phone:

   ```
   ## 1. (our title) · E-YYYYMMDD-nn

   **Theirs:** (account, size) · (link) · (its number), (ratio)× their usual
   (Two lines: what it is, and why it worked.)

   **Our last published piece:** (its picture, to show the new one keeps our look)

   **Ours:** (the picture)
   (The caption or script, in full.)

   **Kept:** … **Changed:** … **Ours alone:** …
   **We expect:** (the number, and when it is read)
   ```

8. **Record the bet** with the reference's link: `dcos bet … --reference <link>`.
9. **Where the owner approves each piece,** send the link to the review sheet and ask for one answer: "yes", or the numbers to change. One message for the whole batch.

## Checks before you call it done

- The reference is a row in `work/outliers.md` with a link and a ratio.
- You kept the mechanism, the shape and the size. No words, picture, story or look of theirs is in the piece.
- Beside our last published piece, the new one plainly belongs to the same channel.
- The piece carries something only this channel has.
- The review sheet shows the pair, and the experiment names the reference.
- You only linked to their post. A screenshot of it stays in a private repo and is never posted.

## Good and bad

- Good: "Theirs: a two-panel cartoon, 3.4× their usual. One everyday object gets bigger in each panel as the unspoken cost grows. Kept: two panels, one object, no advice. Changed: the setting, the object, the joke. Ours alone: the scene is one a reader sent us."
- Bad: the same headline with two words swapped.
- Bad: "inspired by what top accounts do", with no link and no number.

## Known problems

- 2026-11-24: six pieces written without a reference all took the same shape: an imagined scene, a sensible adult, sensible advice. The owner called them bland and asked for this method.

- 2026-11-25: two channels made pieces that did not look like their own published ones: one switched to flat vector art on a dark background, one rendered a realistic animal in place of its round cartoon character. This skill had said to keep the reference's look. Hence step 3 and the third block on the review sheet.
