# Fresh-eyes check

## Use this when

A piece is finished and about to ship. The maker cannot see its own mistakes. The checker is a second agent, or a fresh sub-agent with no memory of the making.

## What the checker gets

Only these: the finished piece exactly as it will ship, the charter's voice and "never" sections, `look.md` with two of its reference pieces, the checks from the skills that made it, and the proven post it is modelled on, if it has one. Not the drafts, the strategy or the maker's reasons.

## Steps for the checker

1. **Look once, as a stranger.** After 5 seconds: what is this, and would you keep going? Write that down first.
2. **Would anyone pass it on?** Name the person a reader would send it to, and why. If you cannot, the piece is dull, however correct it is. Signs of a dull piece:
   - nobody could disagree with it
   - the writer risks nothing and admits nothing
   - it is "imagine that…" where something real should be
   - the answer arrives before the reader has felt the problem
   - it has the same shape as the channel's last three pieces
   If there is a proven post beside it, say which of the two you would rather read, and the one thing theirs has that ours lacks.
3. **Is it true?** Every fact, number and quote has a source you can open (`fact-check` skill). Nothing is invented.
4. **Is it ours?** It sounds like the charter's voice. It breaks none of the charter's "never" lines. **It looks like ours:** put its first, middle and last frame beside two reference pieces from `look.md`, at phone size. Same art style, same characters, same colours and type? A piece that a follower would not know as ours gets `fix`, however good it is.
5. **Is it allowed?** No bait for likes or shares, nothing misleading, nothing copied: no sentence, picture or story taken from the proven post. AI-made content is labelled where the platform asks.
6. **Does it read as written by someone who knows?** Flag these, and ask for a fact in their place. Swapping one word for another is not a fix:
   - details that sound exact but come from nowhere
   - round numbers with no source
   - words that praise the piece itself: honest, simply, truly, powerful
   - "it is not X, it is Y" used as a habit
   - a clever last line that says nothing new
7. **Does it work as shipped?** Open the real preview at phone size. Spelling. Crop. Captions match the sound. The sound is clear. Links open. Then scan for leftovers of the making, where one hit is a fix: an unfilled placeholder such as `[Name]`; an assistant's phrase such as "Here is the revised version"; raw `**` or `##` on a platform that shows them as typed; a link carrying an AI tool's tracking tag.
8. **Is it one for the owner?** Brand deals, hostility, real disasters or victims, legal or medical claims: stop and send it to the owner.
9. **Answer in this shape:**

   ```
   VERDICT: pass | fix | dull | stop
   First impression: (one line)
   Would send it to: (who, and why) | nobody
   Fixes (3 at most, most important first). Quote the exact line that fails:
   1. "…" — what is wrong
   ```

## Steps for the maker

1. Fix only what is listed. Do not rework the rest.
2. **"Dull" is not fixed by changing words.** Go back to the idea: another proven post (`model-a-proven-post`), or another angle (`find-the-angle`). Or drop the piece. A dull piece does not ship.
3. Ask for one more check if the fixes were large. Two rounds at most: then ship it or drop it.
4. Note in the experiment that it was checked, and by whom.

## Checks before you call it done

- The checker did not make the piece and did not see the drafts.
- The checker did not rewrite it. It lists fixes; the maker fixes.
- Every fix quotes the line that fails. If the checker cannot point to one, it is not a fix.
- A "stop" went to the owner, not around the owner.

## Known problems

- 2026-11-25: two pieces passed checks that never compared them with the channel's published work. One was flat vector art where the channel uses storybook illustration; one was a realistic animal where the channel uses a round cartoon one. Hence the look check in step 4.
- 2026-11-24: this check passed six pieces that the owner then called bland. Every fact and line was fine; nobody would have passed them on. Hence step 2 and the "dull" verdict.
