# Script a short video

## Use this when

You are about to make a short video. Write this before any picture or voice is made.

## Steps

1. **Start from the hook** (`hook` skill): the first frame, the first words and the text on screen.
2. **Write the script as rows, one per beat:**

   | Time | What we see | Text on screen | What is said |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | 0–2 s | | | |

3. **Write the silent version first.** Fill only "what we see" and "text on screen". Many people watch with no sound. It must carry the story alone.
4. **Then add the words.** They add to the picture. They do not describe it.
5. **Test every row.** Each one must do one of three jobs: grab, move the story forward, or set up the payoff. A row that does none is cut.
6. **Put the payoff where the hook promised it,** and not before the end, or people leave early.
7. **End so the last frame leads into the first.** A clean loop lets a replay feel natural.
8. **Read it aloud with a stopwatch.** Fix the times from the real reading, not from a guess.
9. **Check it against the plays** for short video on this platform.

## Checks before you call it done

- Covering the "what is said" column, the story still makes sense.
- No row is there only to explain.
- The total time comes from a timed reading.
- The script delivers what the hook promises.

## Good and bad

- Good: a row reads "3–5 s · hand turns the empty bottle over · 'Day 4' · (silence)".
- Bad: a row reads "narrator explains the background for 12 seconds over a still image".
