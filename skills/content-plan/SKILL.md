# Content plan

## Use this when

`strategy.md` says what to bet on, and you need to decide what gets made on which day. The output is `plan.md` and the bets it lists.

## Steps

1. **Start from the strategy.** The weak step, and the one to three bets aimed at it.
2. **Choose two to four themes.** Things the channel keeps coming back to, each tied to something the reader wants (from `research.md`). A theme with no reader need behind it is a hobby.
3. **Write how each format gets made:** which skills, which tools, how long, what it costs. Take the times from real pieces once you have them.
4. **Check it fits.** Pieces per week × time per piece must fit the sessions the channel really has. Cost per week must fit the caps. If it does not fit, plan fewer pieces, not thinner ones.
5. **Fill the calendar for two to four weeks.** Each row has a date, a platform and format, a theme, the proven post it is modelled on (a row in `work/outliers.md`), the play it uses, and the one thing that is different from our usual. Follow the strategy's format sheet.
6. **Keep the mix.** About 4 in 5 rows are modelled on a proven post or use a play that has held. About 1 in 5 tries something untested, from the brief's "plays to try" or from `find-the-angle`.
7. **Give each strategy bet enough rows.** At least 3 pieces, or you cannot read its result.
8. **Reuse on purpose.** A long piece can feed several short ones (`cut-shorts-from-long-video`). Plan them together: in the long piece's row, name the short pieces it must yield, so its script is written with moments that stand alone. Each short is its own bet and must be different enough to stand as its own post.
9. **Leave room.** Keep one slot in five free for something timely.
10. **Stay ahead.** Aim to have a few days of finished pieces waiting, so one bad session does not stop the channel.
11. **Record the bets.** `dcos bet` for each row in the next week, with the number you expect and `--reference` (the proven post's link, or `none: <what you are testing>`). Put the id in the calendar.
12. **Write `plan.md`,** replacing the old one.

## Checks before you call it done

- Every row in the next week has a bet id and a reference.
- No week asks for more than the channel can make.
- Every strategy bet has at least three rows.
- The plan would still work if the owner were away for the whole period.

## Good and bad

- Good: "Tue: short video, theme 'first week', play `open-short-on-a-threat-or-question`, different: the question is asked on screen, not spoken. E-20270112-01."
- Bad: "Post regularly about our main topics."
